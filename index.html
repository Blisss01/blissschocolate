Chocolate with a point of view.

Bliss & Co. began with a simple belief: the best gifts feel personal. We make chocolates in small batches, pairing familiar comfort with unexpected little twists. From our first recipe to every box we send out today, care is the ingredient we never compromise on.

We are a homegrown Indian chocolate brand serving celebrations big and small. Tell us what you are imagining and we will help make it delicious.
